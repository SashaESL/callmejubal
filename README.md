\# 🏥 Call Me Jubal — Medical Interpreter Training



&#x20;"No titles. Just truth. Just learning."



Call Me Jubal is a web-based flashcard app designed for medical interpreters to master terminology in 9 languages.

\## ✨ Features



\- ✅ \*\*9 Languages\*\* — Study medical terms in your native language

\- ✅ \*\*Voice\*\* — Hear English pronunciation

\- ✅ \*\*Study Mode\*\* — Flip cards and rate yourself

\- ✅ \*\*Test Mode\*\* — Multiple choice quizzes

\- ✅ \*\*CSV Upload\*\* — Add your own vocabulary (CSV and Excel supported!)

\- ✅ \*\*Definitions\*\* — English-to-English explanations

\- ✅ \*\*User Accounts\*\* — Save your progress

\- ✅ \*\*Password Reset\*\* — Secure email recovery

\- ✅ \*\*Language Selector\*\* — Switch languages instantly



\---



\## 🚀 Getting Started



\### 1. Register

Create a free account to start studying.



\### 2. Study

Flip through medical and insurance terms.



\### 3. Test

Take quizzes to test your knowledge.



\### 4. Upload

Add your own vocabulary with CSV or Excel files.
